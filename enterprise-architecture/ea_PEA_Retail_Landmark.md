Below is a structured view of **typical activities and responsibilities** for a **Principal Enterprise Architect** at **Landmark Digital** in Dubai**, contextualized for a large retail/digital transformation environment (Landmark Group ecosystem).

---

# Principal Enterprise Architect

**Organization:** Landmark Digital
**Location:** Dubai
**Industry Context:** Retail, E-commerce, Omni-channel, Digital Platforms

---

# 1️⃣ Enterprise Architecture Strategy & Vision

### Core Activities:

* Define and evolve the **enterprise architecture (EA) strategy** aligned with business growth
* Develop **target-state architecture blueprints**
* Establish architecture principles and guardrails
* Align digital transformation roadmap with enterprise strategy
* Lead capability-based planning (business capabilities model)
* Conduct architecture maturity assessments
* Maintain enterprise technology landscape inventory

---

# 2️⃣ Digital & Omni-Channel Architecture Leadership

Landmark Digital typically supports multi-brand retail across physical and online channels.

### Key Activities:

* Design omni-channel integration architecture
* Architect digital commerce platforms
* Align POS, ERP, CRM, WMS integrations
* Lead API & microservices strategy
* Define cloud-native reference architectures
* Ensure scalability for high-traffic retail events
* Oversee mobile app ecosystem architecture

---

# 3️⃣ Cloud & Infrastructure Governance

### Responsibilities:

* Define multi-cloud strategy (AWS/Azure/GCP)
* Establish cloud governance models
* Create landing zone standards
* Define cost optimization architecture
* Architect disaster recovery & business continuity
* Lead hybrid integration strategy
* Review infrastructure modernization initiatives

---

# 4️⃣ Data & Analytics Architecture

### Activities:

* Define enterprise data architecture roadmap
* Establish data governance frameworks
* Design data lake / lakehouse architectures
* Enable real-time analytics pipelines
* Architect customer 360 platforms
* Support AI/ML platform integration
* Define master data management (MDM) strategy

---

# 5️⃣ Application & Integration Architecture

### Key Activities:

* Rationalize application portfolio
* Define integration architecture standards (API Gateway, ESB, event streaming)
* Conduct architecture reviews for new programs
* Lead vendor solution architecture validation
* Define DevSecOps architecture patterns
* Establish reusable architectural building blocks

---

# 6️⃣ Security & Risk Architecture Oversight

### Activities:

* Embed security-by-design principles
* Review cybersecurity architecture alignment
* Define IAM architecture standards
* Support compliance (PCI DSS, GDPR, regional regulations)
* Oversee third-party integration security architecture
* Participate in risk & architecture governance boards

---

# 7️⃣ Architecture Governance & Operating Model

### Core Governance Activities:

* Chair Architecture Review Board (ARB)
* Define Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
* Establish EA standards & policies
* Define solution approval workflows
* Lead technical debt management strategy
* Ensure alignment across product squads

---

# 8️⃣ Stakeholder Engagement & Executive Advisory

### Activities:

* Advise CIO/CDO/CTO on technology strategy
* Translate business strategy into architecture roadmaps
* Present to executive committees
* Collaborate with product, engineering, and operations leaders
* Support M&A technical due diligence
* Influence technology investment decisions

---

# 9️⃣ Innovation & Emerging Technology Leadership

### Activities:

* Evaluate emerging retail tech (AI personalization, IoT, RFID)
* Conduct PoCs and architectural feasibility assessments
* Define GenAI governance frameworks
* Support innovation labs & experimentation
* Monitor industry technology trends

---

# 🔟 Talent & Architecture Community Leadership

### Activities:

* Mentor solution architects
* Define architecture competency frameworks
* Lead architecture forums & knowledge sharing
* Support hiring of senior architects
* Establish reusable reference patterns repository

---

# Typical KPIs for the Role

* % alignment to target architecture
* Application portfolio reduction
* Cloud cost optimization savings
* Architecture review cycle time
* Reuse of enterprise platforms
* Technology risk reduction metrics
* Delivery success rate for strategic programs
