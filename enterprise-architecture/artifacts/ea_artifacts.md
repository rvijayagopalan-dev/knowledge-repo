
📌 END-TO-END ARTIFACTS: STRATEGY → OPERATIONS

I have organized the Enterprise Architecture Artifacts into five layers:

    1. Strategy & Business
    2. Enterprise & Solution Architecture
    3. Design & Engineering
    4. Delivery & Governance
    5. Operations & Continuous Improvement
