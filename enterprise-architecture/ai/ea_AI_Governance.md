# 🏛 Enterprise AI Governance Policy Template

*(Board-Ready | Regulated-Industry Compatible | Scalable Framework)*

This template defines how AI is **approved, built, deployed, monitored, and retired** in an enterprise environment.

It balances:

* Innovation velocity
* Regulatory compliance
* Ethical responsibility
* Operational resilience
* Strategic alignment

You can adapt this into a formal corporate policy document.

---

# 📘 1️⃣ Policy Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

To ensure that all Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems:

* Align with enterprise strategy
* Operate ethically and responsibly
* Comply with regulatory requirements
* Preserve customer trust
* Minimize systemic and reputational risk

---

## 1.2 Scope

Applies to:

* Machine learning models
* Generative AI systems
* Decision automation engines
* AI-enabled customer experiences
* AI APIs exposed externally
* Third-party AI integrations

Covers:

* Internal productivity tools
* Customer-facing AI
* High-risk regulated AI use cases

---

# 🧠 2️⃣ AI Risk Classification Framework

All AI systems must be classified before development.

---

## 🔹 Tier 1 — Low Risk

Examples:

* Internal document summarization
* Developer copilots
* Analytics assistance

Governance:

* Standard review
* Minimal compliance documentation

---

## 🔹 Tier 2 — Medium Risk

Examples:

* Product recommendations
* Pricing optimization
* Customer support AI

Governance:

* Bias assessment
* Monitoring requirement
* Drift detection mandatory

---

## 🔹 Tier 3 — High Risk

Examples:

* Credit scoring
* Fraud blocking
* Medical recommendations
* Regulatory reporting AI

Governance:

* Executive review
* Formal model validation
* Explainability requirement
* Legal sign-off
* Board visibility

---

# 🏗 3️⃣ AI Lifecycle Governance

---

## 3.1 AI Use Case Approval

Before development:

* Strategic alignment documented
* ROI hypothesis defined
* Data readiness assessed
* Risk classification completed
* Exit strategy documented

Approval required from:

* Product owner
* AI platform lead
* Risk/compliance (for Tier 2+)

---

## 3.2 Data Governance Requirements

All AI systems must:

* Use authorized data sources
* Respect data classification policies
* Enforce encryption at rest and transit
* Follow data retention policies
* Ensure data minimization

For PII:

* Explicit consent compliance
* Data residency compliance
* Access logging mandatory

---

## 3.3 Model Development Standards

All models must include:

* Version control
* Reproducible training pipelines
* Documented assumptions
* Bias assessment
* Validation metrics

High-risk models must include:

* Explainability artifacts
* Fairness evaluation
* Independent validation review

---

## 3.4 Deployment & MLOps Controls

All AI systems must have:

* CI/CD integration
* Rollback capability
* Monitoring for drift
* Performance thresholds
* Alerting framework

Tier 3 requires:

* Canary deployment
* Production approval gate
* Post-deployment audit

---

# 🔍 4️⃣ Responsible AI & Ethics Framework

---

## 4.1 Bias & Fairness

Mandatory for Tier 2+:

* Bias testing against protected attributes
* Fairness documentation
* Remediation plan if bias detected

---

## 4.2 Explainability

Required for:

* Customer-impacting decisions
* Regulatory-impacting decisions

Must provide:

* Model logic summary
* Feature importance visibility
* Human override capability

---

## 4.3 Transparency

Customer-facing AI must:

* Clearly disclose AI usage
* Provide human escalation path
* Avoid deceptive automation

---

# 🔐 5️⃣ Security & Access Controls

All AI systems must:

* Authenticate via enterprise IAM
* Log all model access
* Protect training data
* Protect model artifacts
* Prevent prompt injection (for generative AI)

---

# 📊 6️⃣ AI Monitoring & Audit Framework

---

## 6.1 Continuous Monitoring

Track:

* Model accuracy
* Drift rate
* Bias indicators
* Incident reports
* SLA adherence

---

## 6.2 Quarterly AI Risk Review

For Tier 2 & 3 systems:

* Risk reassessment
* Performance evaluation
* Compliance audit
* Incident summary
* Optionality reassessment

---

# 🚨 7️⃣ Incident & Failure Management

If AI causes:

* Incorrect decision
* Customer harm
* Regulatory violation
* Significant revenue loss

Mandatory steps:

1. Immediate containment
2. Model rollback (if needed)
3. Root cause analysis
4. Risk committee review
5. Board notification (Tier 3)

---

# 💰 8️⃣ Vendor & Third-Party AI Controls

Before adopting external AI provider:

* Vendor risk assessment
* Data privacy agreement review
* Model transparency evaluation
* Exit strategy documented
* Lock-in risk assessed

No black-box AI allowed for Tier 3 use cases without explainability.

---

# 🧭 9️⃣ AI Optionality & Reversibility Clause

All AI systems must document:

* Vendor exit path
* Data portability plan
* Model retraining capability
* Deployment rollback process

Irreversible AI architecture decisions require executive approval.

---

# 🏛 🔟 Governance Structure

---

## AI Governance Committee

Members:

* CTO (Chair)
* Chief Data Officer
* Security Lead
* Compliance Officer
* AI Platform Lead
* Legal Counsel (as needed)

Responsibilities:

* Approve Tier 3 use cases
* Monitor AI risk posture
* Review ethical concerns
* Update policy annually

---

# 📈 11️⃣ Board-Level AI Reporting

Quarterly board updates include:

* AI initiative portfolio
* Risk classification summary
* Revenue impact
* Cost savings
* Incidents
* Regulatory exposure
* Model performance trends

---

# 🧠 12️⃣ Policy Enforcement

Policy violations may result in:

* Immediate model shutdown
* Security review
* Disciplinary action
* Executive escalation
