A Principal Enterprise Architect is a senior-level technology leader responsible for defining, shaping, and governing an organization's overall IT strategy, ensuring alignment with business goals. They drive digital transformation, create roadmaps, and provide expert guidance on, for example, cloud-native design and AI integration across the enterprise. 

 +2
Key Responsibilities & Focus Areas:
• Strategic Vision: Developing architecture roadmaps and standards to guide long-term technology investments, including cloud adoption and modernization.
• Governance & Standardization: Defining frameworks, API standards, and, for instance, security protocols to ensure consistency, scalability, and efficiency.
• Solution Oversight: Guiding end-to-end solution architecture, including, for example, complex system integrations and data flow design.
• Stakeholder Collaboration: Partnering with, for instance, executives, security teams, and product leaders to align technology with business objectives.
• Mentorship: Providing technical leadership and, for example, mentoring to other architects and engineering teams. 

 +5
Required Skills and Qualifications: 
• Deep Technical Expertise: Proficiency in, for instance, microservices, cloud-native architecture, and, for example, AI/ML.
• Strategic Thinking: Ability to view, for example, IT infrastructure and business processes as a cohesive, long-term ecosystem.
• Experience: Often requires significant experience in software development, infrastructure management, and, for example, systems design.
