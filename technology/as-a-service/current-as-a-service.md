Here’s a structured overview of **“as-a-Service” (XaaS)** concepts, grouped into **Product-based services** (commercial/industry offerings) and **Theory / architectural service models** (conceptual and computing paradigms).

---

# 🧩 1️⃣ Core Cloud Service Models (Theory Foundations)

### **Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)**

Provides virtualized computing resources (VMs, storage, networking) over the internet.
Examples: Amazon EC2, Microsoft Azure Virtual Machines

### **Platform as a Service (PaaS)**

Offers development platforms, runtime environments, and deployment tools.
Examples: Google App Engine, Heroku

### **Software as a Service (SaaS)**

Delivers complete applications over the internet.
Examples: Salesforce, Microsoft 365
